2021
Static crackled through the radios, but they weren’t just any radios. These were radios that measured the quantity and power of light traveling through space. They were also able to pick up infrared radiation along with gamma rays and other radio waves, but only if they came from space.
One of the men got up and stared at the frequency on the screen. It made strange frequency waves, seeming to jump up and then jump back down before just resting for a few nanoseconds, creating flat areas divided by tall spikes. Some of them were long and some of them were short, but all the long ones were the same length, and all the short ones were the same length.
“This is strangely unnatural,” said the scientist as he presented it to the head of NASA. “We don’t believe this is an accident, but that someone made these on purpose. The pattern of flat areas repeats, but only after a little while.”
“Do you think it’s a message?”
“I think we shouldn’t jump to conclusions, but I do suspect that this is its origin.”
“It looks like Morse code.”
In less than 20 minutes, they had decoded the message.
Do not expand
2029
Sometime in the next month would most likely be another turning point for humanity. At least for the humans who knew. NASA had kept everything undercover; they did not think the public was fit to know.
Scientists had deduced that the civilization was on Alpha Centauri, four light-years away. Which planet, or even if it was from a planet, had yet to be deduced. The light seemed to radiate from the whole system altogether.
Alpha Centauri was four light-years away, so it had taken four years for the light message from Earth to get to Alpha Centauri, and then it would supposedly take another four years for their reply to reach Earth again. It had been about nine years, but soon it was about to be 10.
The message sent was stored in a large data archive along with many other data collections, so that people could remember it when the aliens replied. The message was now displayed on the large TV in the decrypting room, the room that planned on receiving the new message.
The message on the screen read, “We mean peace. We are curious. How do you understand Morse code, and in turn English?”
Half a mile away, a giant radio antenna received a message made of flashes of infrared radiation that had been traveling through space, originally starting off as light, but being stretched as it passed through gas clouds.
We’ve been watching. We understand you. Do not expand.
The message was displayed clearly on the screen. The people in the room stared at it, not saying anything for a while. They had been waiting for nine years to receive this message.
“Do you think that’s a threat, an order, or a suggestion?” asked one of the people in the room after a little while.
“It sounded like a threat because they also stated they had been watching us,” said another guy.

2050
The world had become a better place. The technology sent to them was mostly only to improve computers, but these new computers, which could calculate things in new and very much improved ways, could now fix many other problems. A cure for cancer had been created. Global warming was now increasing at 20% of its rate in 2045. Eco-friendly chemicals that were truly eco-friendly had been created that could decompose plastics into biodegradable tissue. Twice as many mathematical problems had been solved in the last year than in all of human history combined. A computer was put in place of a government system, and with the new technology, it could be unbiased. The average suffering rate had gone from 98% to 5%.
2056:
Earth and Mars had truly become a utopia. Nobody went hungry, nobody suffered, but along with that, nobody was too spoiled. Everyone was viewed equally, and thus treated equally. The computer system took care of almost everything, and humans had free will to do whatever they pleased as long as they followed three rules.
1: An individual may not intentionally attempt to harm another.
2: An individual may not attempt to gain control or power over another individual or individuals.
3: An individual may not go past the asteroid belt.
The rules were simple, and for the most part, people were able to follow them. Some of the big companies still wanted to expand, though. They wanted to explore the outer reaches of space, for people had the technological capability to make it to many other star systems.
Then one day, the company X-tar sent a spaceship with a crew of five beyond the boundary.
