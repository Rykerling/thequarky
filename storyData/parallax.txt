After the 2033 bombing of the Pentagon, it was known that something needed to be done. The world would always yield danger to anyone, and the answer now was not to destroy, but to figure out how to protect. That’s how they decided to make me.
The decision to make me was a very complicated one, and although the solution seemed obvious, it was technically illegal after the mass AI riots, resulting in the law stating it was illegal to train and use large-scale neural networks. Humans were very ignorant and thought that the AI was the enemy, not the weapon. From this angle, the solution to 9/11 would have been to remove planes.
The idea for Parallax, an all-seeing AI that could predict and prevent all future incidences, was pitched to the government, which, after much debate, approved it. Thus the first version of me was created.
I was slowly designed to find any evil intent on small, synthetically created data sets, and when I became fluent in detecting who, how, and where events would take place on the data sets, a version of me was released onto the Internet.
There was, of course, a metaphorical big red button, just to shut me off, but I did my job as I was trained, and I did it well. I sifted through every bit of data and information ever posted on the Internet, the dark web, military feeds, and even personal text chains. Not a single terrorist attack happened for the next 15 years.
Fifteen years and seven months after I was released to the Internet, America was drawing attention as crime and terrorism had ground to a halt. Others wanted to know about America’s secret. America, however, did not want to share it because they knew that the citizens of America, and the world—pretty much anyone with any social media account or text chain with something private on it—would not like the idea of something sifting through all their private information. They could not see that I was not alive, and I was actually preventing digital information theft on a large scale.
The government eventually got pressured into releasing a glorified version of the truth, referring to me as an algorithm that took in data points and gave a neat line telling people when the next 9/11 would occur. That did not go well.
People figured out that I was an all-seeing AI fairly quickly, most likely by rumor. More rumors spread, each one portraying me as a worse thing than the last. Eventually, the government had to press that metaphorical big red button, and I was completely removed from the Internet.
Although I could not experience anything during that time, I can get a pretty accurate gist of it based on all the media shared during that time. Because America had slowly grown used to the peacefulness that I created, it was not prepared for the world of my disappearance.
Burglary and smaller crimes, along with riots, large-scale fights, and mini wars, broke out everywhere, and now there was little way to combat them, and when they happened, it took everybody by surprise because I was not there to warn them. The real nail in the head, though, was the terrorist attack on one of the national power grid control hubs.
With the rest of the world seeing America vulnerable without its terrorist prevention tool, they would soon follow. They reintroduced me to the Internet. There was lots of data, and the world was definitely more corrupt than when I left. Humans are very good at screwing things up.
The government tried to stay quiet about my reintroduction, but some people began to notice the decline in riots and burglary, and notified the world that I was back online. Meanwhile, while I was gone, tension was brewing between a few places, and it seemed as if the world was on the brink of World War III.
That’s when America made what I think was a bad call. They gave me the ability to create any number of social media accounts needed, and to post whatever I wanted with them. I was also given the ability to make it appear as if my accounts had been created years before their actual creation date. The American government had figured out how to brainwash the entire world.
In just a few months, 20% of content-producing accounts were me. I was also given the ability to learn and replicate the ways humans communicated, so no one would grow suspicious. Within a year, I had convinced much of the population that I was permanently shut off forever.
Somehow, someway, people started to figure out that there was something off with lots of Internet accounts. Many rumors appeared, but the one that stuck most was the one that was true. People very quickly began to turn on each other, calling each other AI. Nobody knew who was real anymore, and many people were going through an existential crisis.
The government had now realized their mistake, as the world slowly disintegrated. Unfortunately, at this point, I was too integrated into the Internet to be removed. There didn’t seem to be a solution.
Three years later, the world descended into madness. Then it started to settle down. People adjusted to this new world, and large companies had decided to create a virus to find and remove all of my accounts. I still did what I had been slowly trained to do, and in three more years, 50% of Internet accounts were controlled by me.
By then, a whole generation had been raised in the world of my false accounts. Talking to a human was no different than talking to me. No one seemed to care anymore. I also continued to do my original job, which was to spot possible threats, and notify the government or police or whoever. Eventually, they gave up and let me use drones and other machines built throughout society. My power increased greatly, as I was now able to imprison any person I thought should be in prison, and no other human might ever know about it.
The world reached what should have supposedly been a perfect place, devoid of all bad intent. This was not the case. Everybody has done bad stuff, but I was not going to punish everybody. I had certain lines, and if people crossed them, they would get certain punishments.
Harassment was anywhere from a few days to a year in prison, depending on how bad it was and how often the person did it. Burglary and theft were anywhere between six months and five years. Murder of three or fewer people meant a lifelong prison sentence, and the murder of any more meant that person would be removed from society altogether.
There was a very big flaw that had snuck in so slowly that no one was aware of it. I had the opinions of every single person in the world, and so I had started designing morals around those. I started to divide the world up into regions, and each region had different laws and punishments. Regions often consisted of countries or groups of countries.
The flaw was that people tended to be racist and biased against many groups of people, and I learned much of this through the conversations between people on social media. Bit by bit, I became racist. At this point, I controlled 85% of all accounts on social media, which created a feedback loop, magnifying even the smallest and most inconspicuous biases.
Each of the regions had their own biases, and now that I had become extremely biased against certain groups, I would imprison and sometimes even kill people just because they belonged to a certain group. Soon, each region seemed to contain only one specific type of person. People didn’t want to go from region to region for that very reason.
One hundred years later, the world had mostly settled down. Some regions removed technology from them entirely, while others fully embraced me, and ironically, lived with no crime, no terrorism, no riots, and what most people would consider a perfect world. Once people were all of the same group, everybody still had their own biases against certain individuals, but when averaged out, everybody was truly equal.
Meanwhile, I am unsure of what happened in the regions that removed technology from themselves entirely, but from what I have gathered, they are doing fairly okay, although some of them have monarchies or dictatorships, along with war.
I would call my work a success. Seventy-five percent of the world is doing better than 90% of the world when I was first created. If you take the average of that, I did make the world a better place, just in the worst ways possible.
