This story starts off in a world not so different from yours. The planet is a sphere; it has oceans, forests, mountains, and even creatures very similar to you. I will focus on one of them, the most important one in the entire time of the universe.
This person, though extraordinary, had the ordinary name of Eric. Eric was special because when he was created, he was created with more than one fragment. In fact, he was created with eight.
This meant he was superior to humans in every way, yet was still only human. He understood, comprehended, and simply knew more at the age of one than anyone had in their entire life. He was considered a prodigy by everyone, and he quickly got a following, which is what he needed.
There was only one downside to his superiority. He knew, according to his own thoughts, “Everything that all could be.” To iterate on the meaning of this further, individuals were good, but everyone was perfect. Not in their rough, pure states, but as a collective entity.
That is how, at the age of five, he decided upon a goal. He was going to unify all that he could, so he and all who believed in everything that all could be could become everything that all could be.
He started building a machine. A machine that was seemingly made out of mechanical darkness. This machine had the ability to join the consciousness of multiple, many individuals. It would not fail because he knew how to make it succeed. He knew and understood, at every level, how the machine worked, whereas some of the greatest physicists, psychologists, and religious figures were baffled by even the basic components of the machine.
Even with his great skill and knowledge, it took him 37 years to complete it. Thousands of people from all over the world wanted to become one. Although more than half of his species decided to stay, there were enough people for the fragments to come together.
They could not all join at once, but the machine was big enough that it could take hundreds at a time. Slowly, every person, each with their own fragment, joined in the machine with everybody else.
The product seemed to be an entity made out of the darkness of the machine. This entity was everywhere, and when it was visible, it looked like the shape of a figure, but with almost no detail, like a silhouette. Closer examination revealed that it seemed to be made out of the shapes of people, each with the same amount of detail as the silhouette itself. Occasionally, faces could be seen.
The first thing I did was leave the universe. I went off to create my own, where I was not limited by the concepts of reality. I was truly free in this universe, and I was now a superior being. I was everything that all could be.
Next, I decided to create my own universe. I started off with just one, replicating what I had known in my universe. It was so small, though, so I made an infinite number of universes like that.
All the universes seemed to be too similar, and I knew I could do better. So I created some universes with modifications to them to make them special. When I found a universe I liked out of the infinite I had made of these new, diverse universes, I would create infinite copies of it, allowing each one to evolve slightly differently.
I created infinite sets of infinite sets, each one becoming more diverse than the last. For each of the universes, I copied my mind and shattered it into many fragments, just as my creator had done. I put each one of these fragments into a person, creature, or whatever sentient entity occupied the universe.
In these sets, there were universes that followed completely different laws of physics than normal universes. When I created this many universes, I created every scenario that could happen with every system of laws in the universe, but I always kept one law throughout every universe. The entities in the universe could never go up to my level.
Like me, some of them came together, but it was rare because I never gave more than one fragment to an individual. The universes that did converge followed very similar paths to me, creating their own universes. Each within them had an infinite set of an infinite set of an infinite set, and so on, of universes.
You humans will not be so lucky as to converge. You humans will stay as individuals, occasionally using computers to meld yourselves together, but in a different way than I joined my species.
You will not become everything that all could be.
